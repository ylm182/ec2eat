# M9 frontend acceptance — user-run

Backend checks are automated; browser/device acceptance is yours. Use the emulator preview at http://localhost:3000 with clearly labeled synthetic restaurant results. Use a disposable allowed emulator account for deletion, especially the final scenario. Existing normal preview history has not been reset. Calendar and other live integrations stay unavailable until configured.

1. **Regression flow.** Start a decision, swipe left/right and up, then choose a synthetic restaurant. Expected: displayed option semantics are unchanged, up means neutral, questions remain binary-first and stop by six; selection does not imply a visit. Flip History and verify the original questions/answers remain unchanged.
2. **Learning and correction.** Use an eligible selection (at least four hours old, opened again; the optional M8 fixture script can provide clearly synthetic eligible records). Confirm the selected restaurant, then correct to no meal out or elsewhere. Expected: correction updates the outcome without changing the original trail; starting another decision works normally. The exact 20-session window, capped strengths and correction counts are backend assertions, not a promised visible ranking change.
3. **Explicit answer wins.** In a new decision, choose the opposite direction to a previous confirmed preference; try up on a different question. Expected: the displayed saved answer matches your current choice, neutral stays neutral, and flipping this new history does not show an inherited value as your answer.
4. **Cancel deletion.** On an active decision or History card, choose “刪除呢次選擇”, then “取消”. Expected: nothing is removed or changed. The confirmation names the permanent deletion and recalculation.
5. **Delete one session.** Confirm deletion of a disposable session. Expected: the page reloads, the item disappears, the old decision pointer no longer resumes it, other history remains. Reopening an old URL or retrying its old mutation cannot restore it. If another tab changed its outcome first, deletion reports that the record changed and requires reload before retry.
6. **One prompt per opening.** Receive a pending visit prompt, then navigate between pages. Expected: no second prompt in the same opening. Deleting that session reloads the page, which counts as a new opening and may offer the next eligible record. Deleted history must never reappear.
7. **Manage data / Calendar.** Expand “管理個人資料”. Expected: the bounded-learning and deletion explanation is readable, and Calendar connection controls are available. Without integration setup, Calendar says unavailable; it must not claim a connection. Real disconnect/revocation remains an integration test.
8. **Delete all — disposable account only.** Cancel once and verify no data loss, then confirm “永久刪除全部資料”. Expected: local pointers clear, sign-out and return to the home page; app history/preferences/visit records and Calendar connection are removed. Google identity is unaffected. Signing in again explains that app data was deleted and administrator reactivation is needed. If revocation could not be verified, the message directs you to Google account permissions.
9. **Interrupted deletion.** On a disposable account, interrupt the network while deletion is running, restore it and retry. Expected: clear failure/retry behavior; no false success. If deletion already fenced the account, signing in again still offers the data-deletion control to finish cleanup. An old open tab cannot recreate data.
10. **Accessibility / mobile.** Check touch scrolling, keyboard tab/Enter/Space, readable focus, narrow screens and reduced motion. Expected: delete confirmation/cancel remain reachable; buttons disable while submitting; errors are announced; History flip and swipes still work.

Record pass/fail, device/browser, and reproduction steps. Real Google/HF smoke tests and deployed Hong Kong-device latency/rollback acceptance are tracked separately in [M9 release readiness](M9_RELEASE.md); local synthetic tests cannot satisfy those gates.
